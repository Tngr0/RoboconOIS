01 / About us

Curious minds.
Working machines.

OIS Robocon is a hands-on community for students who want to understand how things work and then build something better. Our workshop is where design, engineering, and teamwork meet.

Sketching mechanisms, planning systems, and turning a problem into a buildable idea.

Combining electronics, mechanical systems, and software into one reliable robot.

Testing early, learning from failure, and making every version more capable.
